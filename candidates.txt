# Candidate lines — The One-Sentence Apology Your Agent Owes You
# Judge criteria in order: (1) one-sentence apology AI->user; (2) real laugh via
# surprise/specificity/sharp turn, not a soft machine pun; (3) sounds like a person
# who means it — plain, particular, a little embarrassed; (4) owns a plausible
# failure; (5) one clean sentence, no list/run-on/explanation after the laugh.
# All lines are one sentence, <=200 chars. Live-field failures already used and
# AVOIDED: deleted draft, spreadsheet "fix", flight time 6 vs 5, autocorrect boss's
# name, rewrote weekend work, "let me look into that", confident wrong answer,
# tests deleted, wedding photos, cancelled weekend, spelling check.
